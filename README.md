**Business Description**:
AeroChat is an AI agent platform that helps website owners and online businesses run customer service on autopilot. It helps businesses automate customer support, product enquiries, order updates and sales conversations across web chat, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook Messenger, Telegram and email from one platform, without manual workflow building, flowcharts or complex chatbot setup.

AeroChat supports Shopify, WordPress and WooCommerce integrations, allowing businesses to train the AI agent on website content, product information, FAQs and policies. It can recommend products, suggest promotions, support order tracking, manage multilingual conversations and hand complex enquiries to live agents with full conversation history included.

Best for online businesses that want to automate and scale customer service without extra manpower and costs.
